## Hi there 👋

**Lumina Research** is an AI-focused research initiative dedicated to advancing responsible, transparent, and applied artificial intelligence.  

We publish open research, evaluation frameworks, technical methodologies, and reproducible studies that support developers, organizations, and public-interest technology initiatives across Canada and beyond.

Our goal is to illuminate how AI works, how it can be used responsibly, and how open research can support safer, smarter, and more accessible AI systems.

## 🤖 What We Build

- **AI Research Papers & Frameworks:** Methodologies for transparency, evaluation, governance, and practical AI deployment across real-world domains.
- **Model Evaluation & Benchmarking Tools:** Open-source scripts, datasets, and frameworks for assessing model performance, fairness, safety, robustness, and alignment.
- **Responsible AI Resources:** Guidance checklists, risk matrices, assessment templates, and documentation standards for ethical and accountable AI use.
- **Applied AI Tools & Experiments:** Prototype models, algorithms, and technical demonstrations that explore emerging AI capabilities in writing, automation, analysis, and decision support.

## 🌟 Vision

AI research should be open, rigorous, and accessible.

Lumina Research is committed to building a research ecosystem that supports transparent and explainable AI, responsible and ethical AI practices, open evaluation methods and reproducible science, practical tools that help developers and policymakers understand and apply AI safely, and alignment with broader public-interest technology initiatives.  

We believe open AI research should strengthen—not obscure—how AI systems shape the world.
